# Analysis and Identification of Improvement Areas for Wheel Rim Scratches and Surface Damages

## Overview
This project presents a quality engineering case study focused on analyzing wheel rim scratches and surface damage in an automotive manufacturing environment. The objective was to identify major defect sources through process observation, quality data analysis, and root cause investigation, followed by proposing practical improvement opportunities to reduce defects and improve overall product quality.

## Objectives
- Analyze wheel rim surface defects across the manufacturing and assembly process.
- Identify major causes of scratches and dents using process observation and quality records.
- Prioritize defect sources using quality engineering tools.
- Propose practical, low-cost process improvements to minimize defects.

## Methodology
- Manufacturing process observation
- Quality Control data review
- Defects Per Tractor (DPT) analysis
- Pareto analysis
- Root cause analysis
- Kaizen-based improvement proposals

## Key Findings
- Wheel rim scratches were identified as one of the dominant visual quality defects.
- Multiple process stages contributed to surface damage, including handling, transportation, tyre fitment, and final wheel assembly.
- Several practical improvement opportunities were identified to reduce defects without significant capital investment.
